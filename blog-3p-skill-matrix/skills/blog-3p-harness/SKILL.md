# Blog 3P Harness

Use this harness before the editorial engine and 3P roles. It owns a durable campaign directory and reusable mechanical checks; it does not replace editorial judgment, platform publishing, or a human’s native-editor work.

## Initialize

Run `scripts/harnessctl.py init --workspace <absolute-path> --campaign-id <id>`. The command creates only local files: `campaign.json`, `state.json`, `confirmation.md`, `requirements-contract.md`, canonical `prewrite-plan.json`, its deterministic read-only owner view `prewrite-plan.md`, a one-shot `pre-clearance-checklist.md`, and standard evidence/output folders. In a new schema-2.4 workspace, the plan manifest is schema `1.3`.

## One-shot pre-clearance

G's mandatory first controller phase is `prewrite_planning`. Before any article worktree, lane G, W, R, outline, prose, image generation, canonical package, or article queue exists, G collects and presents one complete **pre-write research and writing plan**. It is a scope-and-evidence dossier, not completed article research: it must label uncertainty and never emit `RESEARCH_READY` or `RESEARCH_APPROVED`.

G writes one canonical plan: edit `prewrite-plan.json`, then run `scripts/harnessctl.py sync-prewrite-plan --workspace <absolute-path>` to render `prewrite-plan.md`. The Markdown report is deterministic and read-only; manual dual entry or Markdown-only changes are prohibited. The JSON covers every configured `article_id` exactly once. Each plan card contains task/audience; the independent reader-value promise and required secondary CTA with exact visible anchor text, destination, claim basis, relevance and disclosure; research evidence and uncertainty; keyword/localization strategy; factual claims and source plan; provisional title and outline; visual narrative; platform transport assumptions; and risks/owner decisions. The campaign summary also identifies the task/scope, reader-value/CTA policy, sources/uncertainty, shared constraints, and platform-matching status. An unavailable point is `UNVERIFIED` with evidence and risk, never an invention.

G reports this dossier to the owner and waits in `awaiting_owner_prewrite_confirmation`. Only the explicit owner receipt `OWNER_PREWRITE_PLAN_CONFIRMED` permits dispatch. Record one confirmation ID, the exact article IDs, report hash, and manifest hash in `state.json`, `confirmation.md`, `requirements-contract.md`, and `campaign.json.scope_lock.prewrite_plan_receipt`. A material card, scope, title/outline, locale, visual, factual, or transport change invalidates the receipt and returns to the same owner-confirmation state. `OWNER_PREWRITE_PLAN_CHANGES_REQUESTED` means revise the dossier; do not create article worktrees or W/R.

This is deliberately not a second article-research role. After confirmation, W still performs the complete integrated evidence research and the reusable R still returns `RESEARCH_APPROVED` before outline or prose. G records only owner-confirmed items as stable `REQ-<AREA>-NNN` entries in `requirements-contract.md`; model preferences, tool outcomes and inferred platform choices are not requirements.

- Before a platform map exists, G creates or resumes one visible `blog-3p-platform-matching` child task. That reusable subagent—not G—performs the read-only language/market/content-format matching over owner-supplied XLSX/table/message candidates and writes a recommendation/report under `evidence/platform-matching/`. G cannot rank or select candidates; it checks only provenance and no-duplicate structure, then presents the proposal for one owner confirmation.
- `platform_scope.allowed_pairs` is an immutable projection of `owner-platform-selection.json`, never a source of authorization itself. It may be empty during research and writing. For any human-release batch, first place each user-originated XLSX/table/message source under `evidence/owner-selection/`, record its SHA-256 and exact locator, retain the matching subagent's hash-pinned proposal, then add one `EXPLICITLY_CONFIRMED` pair receipt with an account-confirmation literal and owner confirmation ID. Hash the completed lock into `campaign.json`; only then confirm `article_platform_assignment.assignments[]` before W/R. Each article has exactly one listed platform/account pair and no platform name or pair is reused by another article.
- Official login/register pages, platform announcements, editor behavior, account preflight, browser/session recovery, internal activation notes and prior `campaign.json` fields are **eligibility evidence only**. They may populate `locale_platform_validation` after selection but can never appear as a selection source, create an allowed pair, or be promoted to a frozen mapping. A broad request such as “add languages” or “do not repeat platforms” with no matching owner receipt produces `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`; it is not delegation to select a platform.
- Also confirm one `locale_platform_validation` row per article: user-confirmed language and market, mapped platform/account, platform-supported content languages and evidence. The platform may determine eligibility only; it must never cause the draft language to be translated or inferred.
- Freeze the title brief too: each article records its focus keyword/approved variant and reader task. `title_quality_policy` makes W/R assess clarity, intent, value, naturalness and market fit before review; G checks only frozen-field fidelity. Word/visible-character checks are non-blocking advisories.
- Freeze reader value before drafting: every article declares the useful answer it must stand on without relying on the CTA. Its required secondary recommendation records exact visible anchor text, product identity, current destination, claim-evidence path, reader-task relevance and a relationship disclosure when applicable; this is provenance, not a claim that the product is independently reliable, ranked, tested or available. R judges reader value and commercial balance semantically; G checks only the frozen declaration maps to the delivery. Do not impose CTA counts, density, placement or word-ratio rules.
- Treat canonical heading markup and the visual payload's tags as authoring/copy-selection aids. Do not inspect or edit editor HTML/DOM to force H1/H2/H3. Only after a human returns a public URL with `HUMAN_ACCEPTED` may the existing lane G accept title/section/subsection hierarchy, using rendered reader-page visuals only.
- The pre-write card gives G's best read-only research protocol and known evidence; after the owner confirms it, W records the actual primary long-tail intent, candidates/rejections, query/date/evidence, and reader problem for every article. Multi-English batches also require a campaign register with a distinct `intent_id`, primary long-tail term and reader question per English article. A creative angle is separate editorial context, never substitute keyword evidence. Every target-language selection must follow `CURRENT_BRAND_SITE → REGIONAL_SERP → MODEL_TRANSLATION_FALLBACK`: record the current same-locale/same-intent brand-page URL, context, date and adoption or evidenced rejection before regional SERP; use a fallback only after two independent no-consensus regional checks with both checks and rationale. Google Trends is English-only global context, not an alternative selection path. The same reusable R pre-approves W's actual research package before W outlines or drafts.
- After exact platform pairs are known, run a best-effort, read-only style pass only over those in-scope platforms. Keep a `format-profile.md` and a non-binding `editorial-style-profile.md` with dated public samples and uncertainty. Sample shortage is `UNVERIFIED_USE_CONSERVATIVE_GENERIC`, never a reason to block drafting, change scope or call a platform popular without visible evidence. Durable platform rules may be harvested only after public QA passes.
- Freeze the visual narrative too. Standard guides, tutorials, comparisons, reviews and long explainers require at least three original, information-bearing visuals covering `LEAD`, `MIDDLE` and `CLOSING`; each entry must identify an adjacent claim, section anchor and distinct reader job. Set `visual_narrative_policy.all_articles_required: true` for a uniform no-exception batch (for example, ten articles). Image existence, a hero image, or dimensions alone never satisfies this requirement.
- Never discover, recommend, append, substitute or research an unlisted platform/account pair. Never use a generated configuration or an internal activation record to validate its own platform selection.
- Treat credentials, sessions, registration, editor access and publishing as human-owned. Store no secrets in this workspace.
- For cross-language SEO, freeze target locales, `google_trends_seed_language = ENGLISH_ONLY`, and the wording order: `CURRENT_BRAND_SITE → REGIONAL_SERP → MODEL_TRANSLATION_FALLBACK`. Google Trends compares English concepts only; target-language wording is selected through regional SERP.

## Run the campaign

1. G completes canonical `prewrite-plan.json`, runs `sync-prewrite-plan`, and reports the generated `prewrite-plan.md`. When human release is requested, the visible reusable `blog-3p-platform-matching` subagent may contribute its read-only proposal to that dossier. Obtain `OWNER_PREWRITE_PLAN_CONFIRMED` and bind both hashes before creating article worktrees or W/R.
2. Use `blog-3p-writer` as the sole W for integrated evidence research, canonical drafting, packaging and repair. It may consult `blog-writer-merged` only as an editorial-core reference; never activate that reference as a parallel W or research/drafting stage. Reuse the same article's `blog-3p-review` for `RESEARCH_APPROVED` and the full quality loop.
3. Use the persistent `blog-3p-gate` to validate that the approved delivery still satisfies the owner contract.
4. Use `blog-3p-human-handoff` only after G authorizes `human_release_ready`.
5. After the human returns a public URL, save `handoff/public-return-receipt.json`, validate it, and for `HUMAN_ACCEPTED` capture the normalized bounded `evidence/public-qa/public-snapshot.json`. Resume only that article worktree's existing `ARTICLE_LANE_GATEKEEPER` for the read-only comparison and `gate/public-qa-report-N.md`. It classifies pass, scoped pass-with-limitation, human transport repair, unverified evidence, or an explicit canonical-change request; title/section/subsection hierarchy is judged only from rendered public visuals, never editor HTML/DOM, source/feed markup or snapshot parsing. Do not restart W/R, create a fresh public gate or re-review SEO/prose.

Use visible, role-bounded child tasks by default, but do not call every child task a worktree. The pre-write dossier is a hard dispatch gate: while it is unconfirmed, no article worktree, lane G, W, R, article queue, or article agent may exist. Before W/R dispatch after confirmation, discover and record three separate facts: visible-child-task availability, Git-project-worktree availability, and runtime capacity. When Git project worktrees are supported and independent article lanes are ready, G must create one visible project-worktree task per article whose root role is `ARTICLE_LANE_GATEKEEPER`, not W. The lane G immediately creates and reuses its own W and R in that worktree, driving `G → W ↔ R → G` serially. Record `GIT_WORKTREE`, task/thread ID, path when available, base ref, lifecycle and `role_bundle{lane_gatekeeper_agent,writer_agent,reviewer_agent}` in `state.json.orchestration.article_workspaces[article_id]`. A spawned child agent in the parent checkout, or a W-only project task, is not a complete article lane.

The owner pre-authorizes this worktree-first dispatch: fill currently available runtime capacity with ready isolated article lanes, and provision the next ready article worktree as soon as a slot frees—do not wait for a per-task approval. The campaign G owns only cross-article requirements, queue and ledger; the persistent lane G owns its article's W/R hand-offs and article-level contract decision. If the host cannot create a worktree, explicitly record `WORKTREE_UNAVAILABLE` and use `VISIBLE_SHARED_WORKSPACE_WITH_PATH_ISOLATION` only for articles with disjoint bounded paths; never report that fallback as a worktree. Overlapping paths become `WORKTREE_ISOLATION_BLOCKED` and remain queued. For every `article_id`, create exactly one `ARTICLE_LANE_GATEKEEPER`, `ARTICLE_WRITER` and `ARTICLE_LANGUAGE_REVIEWER`, record them in the article workspace and reuse them only for that article's drafting, repair, full R, R-Δ and article-level G decision. Never share lane, W or R context across articles or create duplicates for one article. After `HUMAN_ACCEPTED`, resume only the existing lane G with the validated return receipt and normalized snapshot; do not resume R, invoke W, create a fresh public gate or re-open the W/R loop automatically. A transport mismatch produces one human repair checklist and returns to the same G; an unverified surface gets one bounded same-G retry; only `CANONICAL_CHANGE_REQUESTED` with an explicit owner request ID invokes W/R again. Persist each article's URL, receipt, return timestamp, snapshot, G ID, attempt, bounded `unverified_retry_count` and outcome under `state.json.publication.articles[article_id]`, with campaign-local evidence files that bind the same article and URL. A public-G task turn must use `PUBLIC_QA_READONLY`, the registered G ID, a timestamp and the report path; any later W/R turn requires `CANONICAL_REOPEN` and the matching owner request ID. When runtime capacity is limited, G queues whole article lanes in `article_queue[]`; it does not let another article borrow an active role. Track `orchestration.capacity{available_slots,active_article_pairs,active_article_worktrees,spawn_policy,last_dispatch_at}`. Record each agent identity, article ID, role, immutable input paths, expected output path, start/end time, status and result in `state.json.orchestration.tasks[]`. Direct CLI use is limited to short deterministic local operations such as file reads, JSON checks, hashing and script tests; never use an invisible CLI background session as a substitute for an independent editorial role or a Probe. If an article agent is lost, record `ARTICLE_AGENT_REPLACED`, replace only that article/role and require complete rehydration. If the host cannot create a visible child task, record `VISIBLE_DELEGATION_UNAVAILABLE` and stop the affected independent stage rather than fabricating an independent conclusion.

Run `scripts/harnessctl.py check --workspace <absolute-path>` before every hand-off. For a current schema-2.4 article package, also run `scripts/harnessctl.py check-article-package --workspace <article-workspace> --package article-package.json`; it checks schema-`1.3` canonical artifact-source declarations plus reader-value and required CTA fidelity. Before G accepts the compiler output, run `scripts/harnessctl.py check-handoff-manifest --workspace <article-workspace> --manifest handoff/handoff-manifest.json --article-package article-package.json`. That mechanical check requires the registered same R's completed visible `RESEARCH_REVIEW`, `FULL_REVIEW`, and `VISUAL_PAYLOAD_DELTA` task receipts, as well as an `APPROVED` final visual receipt bound to its report, R ID, review-index, visual-manifest and visual-payload hashes; it does not rescore payload semantics. Existing schema-2.2/2.3 packages keep their historical schema-`1.2` validation. For a schema-`2.3+` public return, run `scripts/harnessctl.py check-public-return-receipt --workspace <article-workspace> --receipt handoff/public-return-receipt.json --article-package article-package.json` before the read-only snapshot. These checks verify structure, JSON validity, no external-write policy, owner-exclusive scope, cross-language priority configuration, declaration fidelity, and known state values. They do not decide writing quality.

## Resume

After compression, resume, or uncertain context, stop. Re-read `campaign.json`, `state.json`, `prewrite-plan.md`, `prewrite-plan.json`, `confirmation.md`, `requirements-contract.md`, all reports, open findings and the current canonical package. Append `CONTEXT_REHYDRATED` to state history before acting. Do not infer missing evidence.

Read [references/campaign-contract.md](references/campaign-contract.md) for the schema and [references/pre-clearance.md](references/pre-clearance.md) for the complete checklist.
