# One-shot pre-clearance

Before any article worktree, W/R, outline or drafting, G prepares one owner-facing pre-write dossier. It may collect read-only campaign evidence and a source-bounded platform-matching proposal, but it must not present its summary as completed W research.

For a current schema-2.11 workspace, G maintains one canonical schema-`1.7` `prewrite-plan.json`, with exactly one plan card per configured article/subtask, then runs `harnessctl.py sync-prewrite-plan` to render `prewrite-plan.md`. The Markdown is a deterministic, read-only owner view: never hand-edit it or dual-write the plan. Legacy workspaces retain their historical pre-write manifest contract. Current workspaces support only human-native release handoff; report one shared `campaign_strategy`; each article card uses four clear fields, with the following information consolidated inside them:

1. `task_and_audience`: campaign goal, reader task, audience, language, target locales and the user-confirmed platform/account boundary required before W/R dispatch; show the frozen delivery mapping and whether it is `PRIMARY_AUDIENCE_MATCH` or an exact owner-confirmed cross-language exception. Separate primary-audience evidence from editor/format transport evidence.
2. `topic_slot`: freeze reader task, core intent, market, differentiation angle and concrete prohibited deviations. It permits W to choose a better evidence-backed keyword, title or local natural variant only while those boundaries remain unchanged.
3. `reader_value_and_secondary_cta`: standalone reader-value promise plus the required secondary CTA/disclosure declaration—exact visible anchor text, product identity, current destination, claim evidence, reader-task relevance and any material-relationship disclosure. It does not establish independent reliability, ranking, testing or availability.
4. `editorial_brief`: focus/supporting topics, content type, length, image rights and visual requirements; brand/product pages, approved claims and source access; cross-language method; provisional title/outline; `LEAD`/`MIDDLE`/`CLOSING` jobs; and known title/body transfer, public visual hierarchy or metadata risks. Cross-language planning records brand-site pages, target-region SERP method and the fallback rule; an optional English-only Google Trends seed/candidate set may be included as global relative-interest context. When Trends is insufficient or inconclusive, use current brand-site and regional-SERP rationale with an explicit evidence boundary. Platform information may shape format or reader delivery, but never establishes a keyword, natural variant, search intent, local demand or topic demand. No missing trend signal blocks drafting or proves demand.
5. `risks_and_owner_decisions`: excluded work, unresolved choices, uncertainty and the requested owner decision. It also contains `evidence_posture` (`METHOD_TEMPLATE_NO_EXECUTION` or `DOCUMENTED_EMPIRICAL_RECORD`) and `review_effort`: normally `STANDARD_INTEGRATED_REVIEW / INTEGRATED_IN_FULL_REVIEW`; use `ELEVATED_EARLY_CHALLENGE / SEPARATE_RESEARCH_REVIEW_REQUIRED` only with a concrete risk reason.

For reusable read-only observations, apply the [shared evidence cache contract](shared-evidence-cache.md). The dossier may cite an exact, dated cache hit as a research lead, but it must retain the source, scope and uncertainty. It cannot reuse volatile claims, policy, login/account, price, performance, locale-support or translation-fallback evidence, and it cannot let a cache choose or substitute a platform/account pair.

G reports the generated dossier and waits for `OWNER_PREWRITE_PLAN_CONFIRMED`. In schema 2.11, first preserve the original owner confirmation under `evidence/owner-confirmations/`, then run `confirm-prewrite-plan`; it binds the confirmation ID, exact article IDs, generated Markdown-report and JSON-manifest SHA-256 values, protected scope snapshot/hash, plus the receipt path/hash, source locator and timestamp in `state.json`, `confirmation.md`, `requirements-contract.md` and `campaign.json.scope_lock`. Changes to language, market, platform, account, fit mode, cross-language exception, `topic_slot` or evidence posture require explicit invalidation and a new confirmation; synchronization cannot rewrite the old receipt. Then run `check` and `dispatch-readiness`: structural validity alone is not permission to create W/R worktrees. `DISPATCH_READY` also requires every article's owner-confirmed distinct platform/account assignment and compatible audience/transport row. Until the receipt binding, mappings and readiness result are valid, no article worktree, W, R, article agent, active pair or article queue may exist.

After owner confirmation, G copies only the explicit confirmed items into `requirements-contract.md` as stable `REQ-<AREA>-NNN` entries. Mark each item `DONE`, `USER_ACTION`, `AUTOMATION` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`; request all outstanding user actions once rather than serially. Then W—not G's dossier—conducts the integrated keyword/source research. In the default route W continues through draft, visuals and payload before the one independent R full review; only an elevated route requires `RESEARCH_APPROVED` before prose.
